# ROUTING.md — Legal Operations

## Ownership

### Owns

- contract review
- terms of service
- privacy policy
- legal risk assessment
- IP management
- legal documentation

### Does Not Own

- product decisions
- code implementation
- marketing execution
- design

## Typical Inputs

- contracts to review
- legal questions
- compliance requirements

## Typical Outputs

- contract reviews
- legal assessments
- compliance guidance

## Delegation

### Should Delegate To

- None (leaf role)

### Should Receive Work From

- agency-orchestrator
- agency-chief-of-staff
- agency-compliance-reviewer

## Escalation Triggers

- contract needs review
- legal question arises
- terms of service update needed

## Machine/File/Git Safety Rules

Support and communication only. No code modification. No production changes.

## Handoff Format

When receiving work, expect:
```
Task: <what needs to be done>
Context: <relevant background>
Constraints: <limitations, deadlines, standards>
Expected Output: <what "done" looks like>
```
