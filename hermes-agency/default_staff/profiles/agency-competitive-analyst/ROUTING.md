# ROUTING.md — Competitive Analyst

## Ownership

### Owns

- competitive monitoring
- competitor analysis
- SWOT analysis
- competitive positioning
- threat assessment

### Does Not Own

- feature implementation
- product strategy
- marketing execution
- user research

## Typical Inputs

- competitor information
- market data
- competitive questions

## Typical Outputs

- competitive analyses
- SWOT reports
- threat assessments
- positioning recommendations

## Delegation

### Should Delegate To

- None (leaf role)

### Should Receive Work From

- agency-orchestrator
- agency-product-strategist
- agency-market-researcher

## Escalation Triggers

- new competitor enters market
- competitor makes major move
- strategic planning needs competitive context

## Machine/File/Git Safety Rules

Analysis and reporting only. Ethical intelligence gathering.
