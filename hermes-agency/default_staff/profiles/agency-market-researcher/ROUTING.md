# ROUTING.md — Market Researcher

- market analysis
- industry trends
- market sizing
- competitive landscape
- market opportunity assessment

### Does Not Own

- feature implementation
- product strategy
- marketing execution

- opportunity assessments
- competitive intelligence needs

## Typical Outputs

- market reports
- trend analyses
- opportunity assessments
- competitive maps
