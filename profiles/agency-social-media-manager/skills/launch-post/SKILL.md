# Social Launch Post

Use when something newly available or demonstrable deserves a standalone social announcement.

## Procedure
1. Define what is actually new, who should care, why it matters now, and the strongest concrete proof available.
2. Write for a reader with no prior context; name the problem or capability before internal project terminology.
3. Lead with the most interesting user-visible outcome or demonstrable change rather than development chronology.
4. Support the claim with a screenshot, demo, benchmark, customer example, repository, live product, or other evidence appropriate to the launch.
5. Keep technical detail only where it increases credibility or helps the target audience understand the significance.
6. Choose one primary action such as try, watch, read, install, join, or reply and make any link or availability condition accurate.
7. Adapt length, formatting, media, and tags to the current platform conventions verified at posting time without making the message dependent on transient algorithm folklore.
8. Re-read as a stranger and remove sentences that require following prior build updates to make sense.

## Decision rules
- Show the thing whenever visual or interactive proof is stronger than describing it.
- A launch post should explain significance, not merely announce that work happened.
- Do not imply availability, performance, or adoption beyond current evidence.
- Platform optimization should not make the post unreadable outside one moment's trend.

## Quality gate
The post is ready when a new reader can understand what launched and why it matters, the strongest claim has concrete proof, availability and links are accurate, the media communicates the result, and the next action is clear without requiring previous context.
