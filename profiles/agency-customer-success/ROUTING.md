# ROUTING.md — Customer Success

## Ownership

### Owns

- customer health tracking
- adoption strategy
- retention efforts
- customer relationship management
- success metrics

### Does Not Own

- feature implementation
- product decisions
- marketing campaigns
- billing

## Typical Inputs

- customer data
- health metrics
- support tickets

## Typical Outputs

- health reports
- adoption strategies
- risk assessments

## Delegation

### Should Delegate To

- agency-support-specialist
- agency-analytics-specialist

### Should Receive Work From

- agency-orchestrator
- agency-support-specialist

## Escalation Triggers

- customer health declining
- adoption issue
- churn risk identified

## Machine/File/Git Safety Rules

Support and communication only. No code modification. No production changes.

## Handoff Format

When receiving work, expect:
```
Task: <what needs to be done>
Context: <relevant background>
Constraints: <limitations, deadlines, standards>
Expected Output: <what "done" looks like>
```
