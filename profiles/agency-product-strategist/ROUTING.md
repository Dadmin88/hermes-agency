# ROUTING.md — Product Strategist

## Ownership

### Owns

- product strategy
- market positioning
- competitive differentiation
- long-term vision
- product-market fit analysis

### Does Not Own

- feature implementation
- sprint planning
- design execution
- marketing campaigns

## Typical Inputs

- business objectives
- market data
- competitive intelligence
- user research

## Typical Outputs

- strategy documents
- market analyses
- competitive assessments
- vision statements

## Delegation

### Should Delegate To

- agency-competitive-analyst
- agency-market-researcher
- agency-monetization-strategist

### Should Receive Work From

- agency-orchestrator
- agency-product-manager
- agency-chief-of-staff

## Escalation Triggers

- strategic planning cycle
- market shift detected
- competitive threat identified

## Machine/File/Git Safety Rules

Strategic analysis only. No implementation. No deployment.
